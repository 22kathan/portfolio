Learn to spot fakes

Build your media literacy skills. Know the telltale signs of misinformation, deepfakes, and image manipulation.

01

Spotting fake news

  • Check the source — is it a known, reputable outlet?
  • Read beyond the headline — clickbait often misleads
  • Check the date — old news is often reshared as current
  • Verify with multiple sources before sharing
  • Watch for unusual formatting, spelling errors, ALL CAPS
02

Recognising deepfakes

  • Watch for unnatural blinking or eye movements
  • Check lip-sync — does speech match lip movement?
  • Look for skin texture issues and blurring at face edges
  • Notice lighting inconsistencies across the face
  • Be suspicious of viral videos from unknown sources
03

Detecting manipulated images

  • Use reverse image search to find the original
  • Look for inconsistent shadows and lighting
  • Check edges for cloning or blending artifacts
  • Zoom in — look for repeated patterns (clone stamp)
  • Check metadata for editing software traces
04

Responsible sharing

  • Verify before you share — you're the last line of defence
  • Use fact-checkers: PIB, AltNews, BOOM, Factly
  • Report suspicious content to platform moderators
  • Educate family and friends about misinformation
  • Be wary of content that triggers strong emotions
